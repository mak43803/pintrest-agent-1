### Strict Typography Technical Specifications

#### Level 2 (Headline)
- **Font**: Elegant Serif Bold (Georgia / Playfair Display)

- **Maximum Lines**: Strict maximum 2 lines
- **Size Range**: 34 pt – 42 pt (Default: 36 pt)
- **Color**: `#1A1A1A` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Strict Prohibitions**: Never use shadows. Never use outlines. Never use gradients.
- **Line Spacing**: 90% – 100%

#### Level 1 (Floating Top Badge)
- **Font**: Segoe UI Bold (Sans-Serif)
- **Format**: ALL-CAPS (Uppercase)
- **Size Range**: 14 pt – 20 pt (Default: 18 pt)
- **Letter Spacing**: 80 – 120
- **Container**: Smooth Rounded Pill Frame

#### Core Composition & Whitespace Principles
- **Strict Element Limit**: Maximum 3 text elements per pin (Top Floating Badge + Headline Title + CTA Button).
- **Generous Whitespace**: Maintain clean breathing space around all text blocks and card margins.
- **Zero Product Overlap**: Text must NEVER touch or obscure the hero product image.
- **Capitalization Discipline**:
  - Floating Top Badge: Uppercase (`ALL-CAPS`).
  - Headline Title: Clean Editorial Title Case.
  - CTA Button: Clean Title Case.
- **Visual Feel**: Calm, premium, effortless, and uncluttered. Mobile legibility always takes priority over decorative clutter.
- **Font Limit**: Strictly maximum 2 font families (1 Serif + 1 Sans-Serif). Zero script fonts.
